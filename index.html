The Vision Behind the Lens


Based in Delhi-NCR, I am a storyteller at heart. Whether framing a cinematic sequence, capturing a candid wedding moment, or directing the flow of a choreography set, my goal is to distill raw emotion into visual art.

My journey spans across independent short films like The Flow of Remembrance and Sapno Ka Ghar, high-end wedding cinematography, and stage choreography. By blending the rhythm of dance with the composition of a camera, I bring a unique, dynamic perspective to every project.

Services & Packages
